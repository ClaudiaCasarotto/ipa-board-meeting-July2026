Donors Dinner
Naureen Karachiwalla
IFPRI, Research Fellow

Naureen Karachiwalla is a Research Fellow in the Poverty, Gender, and Inclusion Unit. She is also a Non-Resident Fellow at both the Consortium for Development Policy Research (CDPR) and the Centre for Economic Research in Pakistan (CERP). Naureen's research uses experimental and quasi-experimental methods to examine how public policy design can improve public service delivery and reduce poverty. She studies the role of career incentives in improving the quality of public services; the design, implementation, and market effects of public programs in reducing poverty; and the role of public and private investments in human capital with regards to education, mental health, and nutrition.

Monica Blagescu
British Council, Head of Programmes

Monica is the Head of Programmes at the British Council Kenya, where she leads work on education, innovation, entrepreneurship, and science. Before moving to Kenya, she was Director of Programmes at the UK's Disasters Emergency Committee and Director of the Ethiopia-Canada Cooperation Office. Monica has dedicated a large part of her 25-year career to promoting community voice and accountability, improving access to opportunities for women and young people, and fostering stronger linkages between research, policy, and practice.

Victor Wambua
Mastercard Foundation, Regional Leader A2F

Victor Wambua is an experienced finance professional with a robust background in investment management and risk analysis. Currently serving as the Regional Lead for Access to Finance at Mastercard Foundation since February 2023, Victor previously held multiple roles at DEG from April 2016 to April 2023, culminating in the position of Vice President. Prior to that, Victor worked at Centum Investment Company Limited from August 2012 to March 2016, where responsibilities included managing substantial funds and investments across East Africa. Early career experience includes a role as a Senior Risk Analyst at KPMG Kenya and an internship at Centum focusing on risk management. Victor holds a BSc in Actuarial Science from the University of Nairobi.

Serah Nderitu
Global Innovation Fund, Managing Director Grants

Serah brings more than 15 years' experience working with innovators, researchers, and policy makers in the climate change sector. She has worked on entrepreneurship and innovation programmes across Africa, including at the Global Centre on Adaptation (GCA), where she managed a $7M programme supporting innovators developing climate adaptation and resilience solutions.

Serah served as a climate specialist at the Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF), leading youth initiatives and overseeing a $25 million climate programme. As a science and innovation manager for the Foreign Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), she supported over 500 innovative start-ups and researchers. Previously, at Kenya Climate Innovation Centre (KCIC) she led on policy advocacy and advised over 100 start-ups developing climate technologies.

Michael Musyoki
FCDO, Education Lead

Michael leads the basic education policy and programme engagement at FCDO including government and stakeholder management.

Philile Shongwe
Livelihood Impact Fund, Senior Manager, Research & MEL

Philile leads the design and implementation of the Eyeglasses Initiative's learning agenda at Livelihood Impact Fund, including designing M&E systems, dashboards, and feedback loops to inform how partners in eye health learn and scale.

Radha Karnad
CIFF, Director, Maternal, Child Health and Nutrition

Dr Radha Karnad is a health systems specialist with over 15 years of experience in strengthening health systems and in designing and implementing primary care and women’s health programs across East and Southern Africa. Radha is the Director, Child Health and Development – Africa at the Children’s Investment Fund Foundation, overseeing CIFF’s investments in maternal, newborn, and child health and nutrition across Africa. She was formerly a founding team member of HealthX Africa – a digital-first primary health care service providing affordable care across Kenya. Before HealthX, Radha established the global Primary Health Care (PHC) portfolio as Senior Technical Advisor for PHC at Jhpiego Corp. She has formerly worked as Program Manager, focused on quality maternity care programming, at Jacaranda Health. An NHS-trained doctor, Dr Karnad has a Bachelor of Arts (Biology and Anthropology) from Bryn Mawr College, a Bachelor of Medicine from the University of Southampton, and a Masters in Public Health from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine.

Kerubo Okioga
Porticus Africa, Regional Director

Kerubo is the Regional Director at Porticus Africa.

Kathryn Toure
IDRC, Regional Director, Eastern and Southern Africa

Kathryn Toure started her career in New York City in refugee resettlement before working for the Centre for International and Comparative Studies at the University of Iowa. She moved to West Africa where she worked with Africa Online, the Educational Research Network for West and Central Africa, and eventually as director of IDRC’s West and Central Africa regional office. She is author, co-author, and co-editor of several books and papers, and a member of the editorial board of Langaa Research and Publishing Common Initiative Group, which publishes 50 books about African countries annually. Kathryn has a PhD in Education, with an emphasis in sociocultural studies, from the University of Montreal.
