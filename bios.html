Donor representatives' profiles

Donors Dinner
Tewodros Aragie Kebede
Norad, Senior Adviser, Knowledge and Innovation

Tewodros is as a Senior Advisor for the ⁠Norwegian Agency for Development Cooperation, focusing on analytics and international development strategies. Before joining Norad he was a Senior Researcher at the Fafo Institute for Labour and Social Research in Oslo, Norway. His work focuses on survey methods, development economics, and program evaluations, with a regional emphasis on the Middle East and Africa. He has contributed to initiatives such as the World Development Report on Jobs. Tewodros holds over 15 years of experience in international development and has provided technical assistance to statistical bureaus and NGOs.

Naureen Karachiwalla
IFPRI, Research Fellow

Naureen is a Research Fellow in the Poverty, Gender, and Inclusion Unit. She is also a Non-Resident Fellow at both the Consortium for Development Policy Research (CDPR) and the Centre for Economic Research in Pakistan (CERP). Naureen's research uses experimental and quasi-experimental methods to examine how public policy design can improve public service delivery and reduce poverty. She studies the role of career incentives in improving the quality of public services; the design, implementation, and market effects of public programs in reducing poverty; and the role of public and private investments in human capital with regards to education, mental health, and nutrition.

Monica Blagescu
British Council, Head of Programmes

Monica is the Head of Programmes at the British Council Kenya, where she leads work on education, innovation, entrepreneurship, and science. Before moving to Kenya, she was Director of Programmes at the UK's Disasters Emergency Committee and Director of the Ethiopia-Canada Cooperation Office. Monica has dedicated a large part of her 25-year career to promoting community voice and accountability, improving access to opportunities for women and young people, and fostering stronger linkages between research, policy, and practice.

Rouja Johnstone
FAO, Agribusiness Specialist

Rouja is an Agribusinss Specialist with the Investment Center at the FOod and Agriculture Organization (FAO). She has over 25 years of experience in international development in Africa, Asia and Europe, working directly for or in partnerships with government, private sector and fianciers. In the last ten years, under the FAO Investment Centre, she supported over 50 IFI investments through designs, supervision or evaluation – World Bank Group, IFAD, AfDB, GCF, EU and FAO in agriculture, forestry and fisheries sector development.

Victor Wambua
Mastercard Foundation, Regional Leader A2F

Victor is an experienced finance professional with a robust background in investment management and risk analysis. Currently serving as the Regional Lead for Access to Finance at Mastercard Foundation since February 2023, Victor previously held multiple roles at DEG from April 2016 to April 2023, culminating in the position of Vice President. Prior to that, Victor worked at Centum Investment Company Limited from August 2012 to March 2016, where responsibilities included managing substantial funds and investments across East Africa. Early career experience includes a role as a Senior Risk Analyst at KPMG Kenya and an internship at Centum focusing on risk management. Victor holds a BSc in Actuarial Science from the University of Nairobi.

Serah Nderitu
Global Innovation Fund, Managing Director Grants

Serah brings more than 15 years' experience working with innovators, researchers, and policy makers in the climate change sector. She has worked on entrepreneurship and innovation programmes across Africa, including at the Global Centre on Adaptation (GCA), where she managed a $7M programme supporting innovators developing climate adaptation and resilience solutions.

Serah served as a climate specialist at the Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF), leading youth initiatives and overseeing a $25 million climate programme. As a science and innovation manager for the Foreign Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), she supported over 500 innovative start-ups and researchers. Previously, at Kenya Climate Innovation Centre (KCIC) she led on policy advocacy and advised over 100 start-ups developing climate technologies.

Benjamin Piper
Gates Foundation, Director, Global Education

Dr. Benjamin leads the foundation’s effort to improve foundational literacy and numeracy in low- and middle-income countries. Before joining the foundation, Benjamin was the senior director for Africa education for RTI International, where he provided support to large-scale education projects across sub-Saharan Africa, the Middle East, and Asia. Earlier, he was the chief of party for the Kenyan national literacy program Tusome, a set of RCTs in Kenya called PRIMR, and Kenya’s National Tablets Programme. He was the principal investigator for Learning at Scale, a multi-country study of highly effective large-scale education programs, and for Science of Teaching, an effort aimed at increasing knowledge about the technical details of how to improve pedagogy at large scale. Benjamin has a doctorate in international education from the Harvard Graduate School of Education.

Samburu Wa-Shiko
Gates Foundation, Regional Representative

Samburu is the Regional Representative, East Africa at the gates Foundaiton. He is an international professional with 10 years of rich global experience. Before joining the Gates Foundaiton, he has worked at the World Economic Forum (Geneva) and multiple United Nations agencies – including UNHCR (Geneva and Chad), the UN Department of Political Affairs (New York) and UN Human Rights Office (Geneva). Additionally, he has cutting-edge private sector consulting and NGO experience spanning across 15 countries in Europe, North America and Africa. He has also run large humanitarian programmes, managed cross-cultural teams and directed relationships with donors. He is currently a PhD candidate at the University of Manchester, England.

Philile Shongwe
Livelihood Impact Fund, Senior Manager, Research & MEL

Philile is an international development leader and social innovator with a deep commitment to using data-driven methods and social innovation to improve lives in a measurable way. Over the past several years, she has designed evaluations, managed multi-country teams, and supported large-scale global health and social protection programs across Africa and South Asia. In her role as a senior Manager at the Livelihood Impact Fund, she leads the design and implementation of the Eyeglasses Initiative's learning agenda, including designing M&E systems, dashboards, and feedback loops to inform how partners in eye health learn and scale.

Radha Karnad
CIFF, Director, Maternal, Child Health and Nutrition

Dr. Radha is a health systems specialist with over 15 years of experience in strengthening health systems and in designing and implementing primary care and women’s health programs across East and Southern Africa. Radha is the Director, Child Health and Development – Africa at the Children’s Investment Fund Foundation, overseeing CIFF’s investments in maternal, newborn, and child health and nutrition across Africa. She was formerly a founding team member of HealthX Africa – a digital-first primary health care service provider in Kenya. Before HealthX, Radha established the global Primary Health Care (PHC) portfolio as Senior Technical Advisor for PHC at Jhpiego Corp. She has formerly worked as Program Manager, focused on quality maternity care programming, at Jacaranda Health. An NHS-trained doctor, Dr Karnad has a Bachelor of Arts in Biology and Anthropology and a Bachelor of Medicine, and a Masters in Public Health from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine.

Victor Mbao
IDRC, Senior Program Specialist

Dr. Victor is an animal health scientist with expertise in tropical animal health, veterinary epidemiology, and parasitology. He currently serves as Senior Program Specialist at the International Development Research Centre (IDRC), Canada. Previously, he managed a GALVmed-funded project to upgrade a livestock vaccine manufacturing plant in Malawi and led a Belgian-funded research initiative in Zambia that introduced the country’s first East Coast fever immunization program in the Southern Province. Victor has extensive experience in the research and development of animal health products, international project management, vaccine research, coordination of multi-stakeholder partnerships, value chain analysis, and technology transfer. He holds a Doctorate in Veterinary Science from the Institute of Tropical Medicine (Antwerp) and Ghent University (Belgium).

Kerubo Okioga
Porticus Africa, Regional Director

As Regional Director of Porticus Africa, Kerubo leads a diverse team of 15, driving regional strategy and portfolio implementation with a focus on depth, alignment, and systemic impact. She champions the meaningful participation of people with lived experience across all grants and programmes, and actively fosters collaboration within Africa and across global regions to generate sustainable, evidence-informed change. A committed people manager, Kerubo cultivates a learning culture that prizes curiosity, innovation, and staff growth, while embedding MEL practices at the heart of Porticus Africa's grant-making. She is a passionate advocate for re-imagining African philanthropy centring voice, creativity, diversity, and genuine inclusion.

Leah Mwaura
Rockefeller, Program Specialist

Leah has an exceptional background in the development sector, known for building and motivating program teams to meet project goals and demonstrating leadership in complex project implementation, systemic change, and process improvement. She currently consults for the Rockefeller Foundation and has supported several SMEs access markets and finance. Leah believes strongly in sharing knowledge, bringing extensive research experience and several publications, including a recent SNV-WUR collaboration assessing COVID-19's impact on Kenyan food systems. Among her greatest achievements is linking MFIs and financial institutions to secure lending for smallholder farmers.

Charles Otieno Opiyo
IDRC, Regional Director, Eastern and Southern Africa

Charles is an accomplished Public Policy and Advocacy Specialist driving food systems transformation and human development programming across Kenya and beyond. He has led high-level initiatives spanning the UN Food Systems Transformation Agenda, county-level nutrition programmes, and preparations for the Nutrition for Growth Summit, managing projects on healthier diets and food systems. His expertise covers policy formulation, budget advocacy, and multi-stakeholder coordination. He has also spearheaded humanitarian nutrition interventions in ASAL communities as a trained Core Humanitarian Standards and Sphere practitioner.

Kathryn Toure
IDRC, Regional Director, Eastern and Southern Africa

Kathryn started her career in New York City in refugee resettlement before working for the Centre for International and Comparative Studies at the University of Iowa. She moved to West Africa where she worked with Africa Online, the Educational Research Network for West and Central Africa, and eventually as director of IDRC’s West and Central Africa regional office. She is author, co-author, and co-editor of several books and papers, and a member of the editorial board of Langaa Research and Publishing Common Initiative Group, which publishes 50 books about African countries annually. Kathryn has a PhD in Education, with an emphasis in sociocultural studies, from the University of Montreal.
